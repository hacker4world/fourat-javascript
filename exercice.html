Who is Ronaldo

Cristiano Ronaldo (fully named Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro) is a Portuguese professional footballer widely regarded as one of the greatest players in the history of the game. Nicknamed CR7, he is known for his incredible athleticism, goal-scoring prowess, and work ethic.

Cristiano Ronaldo in Champions League

Cristiano Ronaldo has scored 140 goals in 183 UEFA Champions League appearances, making him the competition's all-time top scorer. He won the tournament 5 times (1 with Manchester United, 4 with Real Madrid) and holds the record for the most goals in a single season (17 in 2013/14).
